Cassiel CRM Privacy Policy

Effective: November 20, 2026

Cassiel CRM ("the App") is a customer relationship management tool used by authorized staff of Cassiel, Inc. (Visiting Angels). The App is hosted and operated by Aptitude AI, a brand of Grisly Manor LLC dba DAS Labs ("Aptitude AI," "we," "us"), on behalf of Cassiel, Inc. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how users can control it.

Who can use the App

The App is available only to staff invited by Cassiel, Inc. It is not offered to the general public.

Roles

Cassiel, Inc. controls the CRM data its staff enter into the App. Aptitude AI hosts and processes that data on Cassiel, Inc.'s behalf, and is responsible for the App's handling of Google user data as described below.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and login credentials for authorized staff.
  • CRM data: contact, referral source, and activity information that staff enter into the App in the course of their work.
  • Google user data: if a user chooses to connect their Google account, the App requests:
    • Basic profile information (name, email address, and email aliases), to identify the connected account and recognize the user on calendar events.
    • Read-only access to Google Calendar events (calendar.events.readonly), to show the user's scheduled meetings alongside related CRM contacts and activities.

The App does not create, modify, or delete calendar events.

How we use Google user data

We use Google Calendar data only to display a user's own meetings within the App and link them to CRM records. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

We do not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models.

Cassiel CRM's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share data only:

  • with Google Cloud, which Aptitude AI uses to host the App;
  • with Cassiel, Inc., which owns the CRM data entered by its staff;
  • when required by law.

Google user data is never transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide the App's features, to comply with applicable law, or with the user's consent.

Storage and security

Data is stored in the United States on Google Cloud infrastructure, with encryption in transit and at rest. Access is limited to authorized personnel. OAuth tokens and application secrets are stored in a managed secret store.

Retention and deletion

CRM data is retained for as long as Cassiel, Inc. needs it for its business purposes. Users can disconnect their Google account in the App's settings at any time. When they do, we stop accessing their Google data and delete stored calendar data and tokens within 30 days.

Users can also revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of your data, contact us at the address below.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
