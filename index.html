Cassiel CRM

Referral relationship management for Cassiel, Inc. (Visiting Angels)

Cassiel CRM is a customer relationship management application used by authorized staff of Cassiel, Inc. (Visiting Angels) to manage referral partners, contacts, and marketing activities.

Google Calendar connection

Staff may optionally connect their Google account so their scheduled meetings appear alongside the related contacts and activities in the CRM. Cassiel CRM requests read-only calendar access. It never creates, changes, or deletes calendar events, and users can disconnect at any time.

Who operates it

Cassiel CRM is hosted and operated by Aptitude AI on behalf of Cassiel, Inc. Access is limited to staff invited by Cassiel, Inc.; the application is not offered to the general public.
